A Final Word

"Therefore, since we are surrounded by such a great cloud of witnesses, let us throw off everything that hinders and the sin that so easily entangles. And let us run with perseverance the race marked out for us, fixing our eyes on Jesus, the pioneer and perfecter of faith."

— Hebrews 12:1-2

The preachers within these pages were not perfect men and women — they were surrendered ones. They held no power but the Holy Spirit, no wisdom but the Word of God, no message but Christ and Him crucified. May their testimonies encourage you that the same God who worked through them desires to work through you.

"The world has yet to see what God can do with a man fully consecrated to Him."

— Words spoken to D.L. Moody by Henry Varley, which Moody took as his life's charge
